Chick-fil-A prices explained

Chick-fil-A is not the cheapest fast-food chain, but its portions and consistency keep the lines long. In November 2026 a basic Chicken Sandwich costs about $5.99, an 8-count of Nuggets about $5.95, and a full meal with a medium side and drink usually lands between $11 and $13 before tax. These are typical U.S. prices. Every restaurant is run by a local operator who sets prices for that market.

Sandwich prices

The original Chicken Sandwich is the cheapest sandwich on the menu. Going Spicy adds about 60¢, and the Deluxe versions with lettuce, tomato and cheese add about $1.50 more. Grilled sandwiches cost more again, about $7.95 for the Grilled Chicken Sandwich and $9.95 for the Grilled Chicken Club. If price is your main concern, the original sandwich and a medium fries is still one of the best-value lunches in fast food.

Breakfast prices

Breakfast is served until 10:30 a.m., Monday to Saturday, at most restaurants. The Chicken Biscuit is about $4.95 and Chick-n-Minis (4 ct) about $5.65. The Hash Brown Scramble Burrito and Bowl are the most filling options at around $6.15–$6.45. See every morning item, with photos and hours, on the full Chick-fil-A Breakfast Menu.

Milkshake and treat prices

All four standard milkshakes cost about $5.15: vanilla, chocolate, strawberry and cookies & cream. Seasonal flavours appear from time to time. For a cheaper sweet fix, the Icedream Cup is about $1.75 and the Chocolate Chunk Cookie about $1.95. Flavours, toppings and seasonal shakes are listed on the Chick-fil-A Milkshake Menu page.

Catering prices

For offices, parties and events, Chick-fil-A offers trays of nuggets, strips and sandwiches, plus salads, sides and gallons of lemonade or tea. Trays are priced by size and are designed to feed groups. Most restaurants ask for catering orders to be placed in advance. Tray sizes, what each one feeds and how to order are covered on the Chick-fil-A Catering Menu page.

Meals vs ordering items separately

A Chick-fil-A meal is an entree with a medium side and a medium drink. Check the app to compare a meal's price with the same three items bought separately. Ordering separately makes sense when you want a different side, a smaller drink, or no drink at all. The meal planner above treats every plate as separate items, so you can see the exact cost of each choice.

Why prices change from one restaurant to another

  • Location: big cities, airports, malls, stadiums and college campuses usually charge more.
  • Local costs: rent and wages are set locally, and prices follow them.
  • Sales tax: it ranges from 0% in a few states to more than 9% in some cities.
  • Delivery: delivery apps often raise menu prices and add their own fees.
  • Extras: extra sauces, cheese, bacon or larger sizes all add to the total.

Five ways to pay less at Chick-fil-A

  1. Order for pickup in the app instead of using a delivery service.
  2. Join the Chick-fil-A One rewards program to earn points on every order.
  3. Share a 12-count of nuggets instead of two 8-counts.
  4. Pick a medium drink or skip it, since drinks add $2–$5 to every meal.
  5. Use the budget planner above before you order for a group.

Chick-fil-A menu price FAQ

How much does a Chick-fil-A meal cost?

A meal with an entree, a medium side and a medium drink usually costs about $11 to $13. For example, a Chicken Sandwich, medium Waffle Potato Fries and a medium Lemonade add up to about $11.59 before tax.

What is the most expensive item on the Chick-fil-A menu?

On the regular menu, the Cobb Salad and Market Salad are the most expensive single items at about $11.25. Catering trays cost more but are made to feed groups.

How much is a 12-count nugget at Chick-fil-A?

The 12-count Nuggets cost about $8.55, or roughly 71 cents per nugget. The 8-count costs about $5.95.

How much are Chick-fil-A milkshakes?

Milkshakes cost about $5.15 each. The four standard flavours are vanilla, chocolate, strawberry and cookies & cream.

Are Chick-fil-A prices higher on delivery apps?

Usually yes. Delivery apps often show higher menu prices and also add delivery and service fees, so ordering for pickup is normally cheaper.

Are these the official Chick-fil-A prices?

No. They are approximate U.S. averages collected for reference. Each restaurant sets its own prices, so check the official app for your location.
